Your receipts stay in this browser.

This version reads receipts and saves expenses, image edits and merchant defaults on your device. It does not upload their contents to the website owner. Your host can receive normal visit information; optional exchange-rate lookup sends the expense date and currencies to the rate provider.

How your data is processed, stored and shared

Local processing and storage. Receipt reading, image editing and export generation happen in your browser. Saved reports and merchant defaults use this browser profile’s storage for this website. They are not automatically synced to other devices or encrypted by this app. Someone with access to your device or browser profile, or scripts running on the same website origin, may be able to access them. Other folders on the same domain can share this storage.

Website owner and hosting provider. This version has no analytics and sends no receipt contents, merchant selections or expense fields to the owner. Hosting providers can receive technical information such as your IP address, browser details, requested files and visit times. What an owner can see depends on their hosting logs and any analytics they add. GitHub states that Pages visitor IP addresses are logged for security. The owner controls the site’s code; added scripts or future changes could change this behavior.

Exchange-rate requests and downloads. Only when you choose historical-rate lookup, the rate provider receives the expense date, source/target currencies and normal connection information; it does not receive the receipt or amount. Downloaded spreadsheets, receipt PDFs and backups are saved wherever you choose and can be read by anyone you share them with. Backups contain both original receipts and edited images; visual markup does not erase originals.

Keeping or clearing records. Save a backup before important changes. Clearing browser data or a new report can remove local records; downloaded files are not deleted. The new-report dialog lets you keep or remove saved merchants and personal defaults. This notice describes this distributed version; your website host’s own privacy terms also apply.
